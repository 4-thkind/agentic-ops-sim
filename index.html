02 diagnosis failure

Everyone has a theory. Nobody has the truth.

The CFO says cut costs. Commercial says spend on customers. Operations blames the supplier. HR says burnout. Five executives, five explanations, zero alignment.

Without AI

It took two weeks of meetings to discover that a cost programme 18 months ago removed 30% of maintenance planners — and nobody connected it to the 41% rise in downtime.

With AI Agents

An operations intelligence agent flags the correlation between planner removal and downtime surge within days. The pattern was always in the data. Nobody was watching.

03 supply chain

The scapegoat was only 30% guilty

A single-source component from Penang: 68% on-time delivery. Everyone pointed at it.

70% of late orders had nothing to do with the supplier

The real drivers were internal: broken scheduling, missing planners, a tech platform nobody trusted. The data was there — it just wasn't connected.

AI Agent: Supply Chain Monitor

Cross-references supplier data with production schedules, maintenance logs, and system accuracy in real time. Doesn't just track what's late — tells you why, before you ask.

04 people crisis

The best people leave first

9 of 31 plant leaders left in 12 months. The exit interviews said the same thing every time:

"My decisions were taken away. I'm measured on things I can't control. I'm firefighting every shift."

Two more critical leaders were about to leave. Nobody in the C-suite knew.

AI Agent: Talent Risk Radar

Monitors engagement signals, exit patterns, and workload metrics. Flags flight risks weeks before resignation — and recommends which conversations to have, with whom, about what.

05 technology trap

$44M on a system nobody trusts

The new manufacturing execution system went live half-migrated. Planners built shadow spreadsheets to survive.

71% actual 95% target

Meanwhile, the one plant NOT on the new system — and still had its planners — ran at 94% SLA. The control group was right there. Nobody asked the question.

AI Agent: System Health Watchdog

Continuously audits system accuracy against outcomes. When planners build workarounds, it escalates the gap and quantifies the business impact in dollars — not dashboards.
